Common questions / 03

Flexible scope. Practical delivery.

The work can be tightly focused or span a wider programme, depending on what is creating the most friction in the operation.

What kind of businesses can this support?

The consulting is suitable for businesses that need stronger control, better systems, clearer process and more useful structure behind the scenes.

Can support focus on one area only?

Yes. Support can focus on a single issue such as stock control or EPOS, or span a wider operational brief.

What is the overall aim?

To help the business feel more joined-up, more commercially aware and easier to manage day to day.

Do you work with Dorset businesses?

Yes. Mackintosh Projects provides business consulting support for Dorset businesses as well as wider UK businesses that need stronger operating systems, commercial control and implementation support.
