About Mackintosh Projects

Practical operational improvement.

Mackintosh Projects helps businesses improve the systems, controls and working structure that support stronger day-to-day performance.

The approach / 01

Improve how the business actually works.

Mackintosh Projects focuses on the areas that usually create operational friction: inconsistent process, weak visibility, poor controls and unclear systems. The aim is to help businesses work with more structure, better discipline and stronger everyday usability.

That can involve support across gross profit, procurement, stock handling, operational systems, EPOS, procedures, training and implementation.
